Connect SAP to every system, partner and device your business relies on, with integrations that are monitored, secure and easy to maintain.

Overview

Most business processes now cross several systems. Orders arrive from e-commerce sites, payments flow through banks, invoices go to government portals and production data comes from the shop floor. Every manual hand-off adds delay and error.

We design and build integrations on SAP Integration Suite and proven SAP interfaces, with error handling and monitoring built in from the start.

What we offer

E-commerce and marketplaces

Synchronise products, prices, stock and orders between SAP and online stores.

CRM and HCM systems

Integrate Salesforce, SAP SuccessFactors and other platforms with your ERP.

Bank and payment integration

Automated payment files, bank statements and payment gateway connections.

E-invoicing and tax compliance

Connect SAP to government e-invoice and e-way bill portals.

Machine and IoT integration

Capture weighbridge, PLC and shop-floor data directly in SAP.

EDI and B2B

Exchange orders, ASNs and invoices with customers and suppliers via EDI.

Frequently asked questions

Which integration tools do you use?

We primarily use SAP Integration Suite (Cloud Integration / CPI), SAP PI/PO for existing landscapes, OData and REST APIs, IDocs and EDI. We choose the tool that fits your landscape and licences.

Can you integrate non-SAP systems?

Yes. Most of our integration work connects SAP with non-SAP applications such as Salesforce, Shopify, banking platforms and custom systems.

How are integration errors handled?

Every interface we build includes error handling, alerting and a monitoring dashboard so failures are visible and can be reprocessed quickly.
