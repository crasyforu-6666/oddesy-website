RISE with SAP Explained: What's Included and What to Ask Before You Sign

A plain-English guide to the RISE with SAP subscription, what it covers, what it doesn't, and the questions that protect you in negotiation.

By the Oddesy Consulting and Services team · 7 Nov 2026

RISE with SAP is SAP's subscription offering for moving to SAP S/4HANA Cloud. It bundles software, infrastructure and a set of services into one contract with one point of accountability. For many organisations it simplifies the move to S/4HANA, but it is important to understand exactly what you are buying.

What RISE with SAP includes

  • SAP S/4HANA Cloud in the private or public edition, licensed as a subscription.
  • Infrastructure hosted on a hyperscaler such as Microsoft Azure, AWS or Google Cloud, or in an SAP data centre.
  • Technical managed services for the infrastructure, database and SAP basis layer, such as backups, patching and system availability, as defined in the contract.
  • SAP Business Technology Platform credits for extensions and integration.
  • Business process tools such as process insights and SAP Signavio capabilities, depending on the package.

What usually remains your responsibility

This is where many surprises happen. RISE does not run your business processes for you. Typically you or your implementation partner still own:

  • Functional configuration, process design and implementation of changes.
  • Application support for users (incidents, how-to questions, enhancements).
  • Custom code, interfaces and third-party integrations.
  • Data migration and data quality.
  • Testing, training and organisational change management.
  • User and role administration, depending on the responsibility matrix.

Always review SAP's roles and responsibilities document line by line, and make sure every task has an owner: SAP, your partner, or your internal team.

Private edition vs public edition

The private edition offers more flexibility: broader functional scope, industry solutions and the ability to extend and modify where needed. It is the usual choice for brownfield conversions. The public edition is more standardised, receives regular SAP updates and suits organisations willing to adopt standard processes with minimal custom code.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. How was our sizing calculated (users and system size), and how do we add users later?
  2. Which tasks in the responsibility matrix are not covered by SAP, and who will do them?
  3. What service levels apply to availability, backup and recovery, and what happens if they are missed?
  4. How many non-production systems are included, and at what size?
  5. What does the migration itself cost, and is it included or separate?
  6. What are the exit and renewal terms, and how will prices change at renewal?
  7. How will we use the BTP credits before they expire?

Is RISE with SAP right for you?

RISE is a strong fit if you want to move to a subscription model, reduce infrastructure management and adopt SAP innovations faster. It may be less attractive if you already have efficient hosting, very specific infrastructure requirements or significant existing licence investments. An independent assessment compares RISE with the alternatives using your real costs.

How RISE with SAP is priced

RISE is a subscription, typically priced on Full Usage Equivalents (FUEs). Different user types count as different fractions of an FUE: a professional user weighs more than someone who only approves requests or views reports. The subscription also depends on system size, the number of non-production systems and any additional services you add.

Before negotiating, build your own user model: list each role, how many people perform it and what they actually do in the system. This avoids paying for more professional-level access than you need.

RISE with SAP vs GROW with SAP

SAP offers two main bundles. RISE with SAP is aimed at existing SAP customers and large organisations, and supports both private and public edition. GROW with SAP is aimed at new, typically mid-sized customers adopting S/4HANA Cloud public edition with a standard, fast implementation approach. If you are already on ECC, RISE is the offering you will usually be discussing.

Paths into RISE

  • Brownfield conversion into private edition: convert your ECC system while moving it into SAP-managed infrastructure.
  • Greenfield implementation: start fresh in private or public edition and migrate selected data.
  • Move existing S/4HANA on-premise into RISE: a technical migration of a system that is already on S/4HANA.

Our guide to brownfield vs greenfield explains how to choose between them, and our RISE with SAP advisory covers the transition itself.

Common pitfalls

  • Assuming RISE means "SAP runs everything". Plan who will provide application support from day one.
  • Undersizing non-production systems, which slows testing and projects later.
  • Letting BTP credits expire unused instead of planning integration and extension work.
  • Signing before the migration plan is clear, leaving subscription costs running while the project is still being scoped.

Frequently asked questions

What is RISE with SAP?

RISE with SAP is SAP's subscription offering that bundles S/4HANA Cloud, hosting infrastructure, technical managed services, BTP credits and process tools in one contract.

Does RISE with SAP include application support?

Generally no. RISE covers technical managed services for the infrastructure and basis layer. Functional application support and changes usually remain with you or your partner.

What is the difference between RISE private and public edition?

Private edition offers broader scope and more flexibility to extend the system; public edition is standardised, regularly updated and suited to organisations adopting standard processes.
