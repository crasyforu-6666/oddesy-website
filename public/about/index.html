Who we are

Oddesy Consulting and Services is an SAP consulting firm that helps organisations implement, modernise and run SAP. Our team combines functional process knowledge with strong technical skills, so business and IT requirements are solved together.

We were founded on a simple idea: clients deserve experienced consultants who give straight answers, plan realistically and stay accountable for results.

Our mission

To make SAP simpler, faster and more valuable for the businesses that depend on it.

Our vision

To be the SAP partner clients recommend first, because we delivered what we promised.
