Delivery approach

A proven path from discovery to run

Every project follows SAP Activate, SAP's standard implementation methodology, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Explore what S/4HANA can do and build the business case.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Set up the project team, plan, governance and system landscape.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Fit-to-standard workshops to confirm processes and gaps.

  4. 04

    Realize

    Configure, build, migrate data and test in sprints.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Train users, rehearse cut-over and go live.

  6. 06

    Run

    Hypercare, support and continuous improvement.
