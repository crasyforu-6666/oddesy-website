Overview

Why it matters

RISE with SAP bundles S/4HANA Cloud, infrastructure, technical managed services and business process tools in one subscription. It can simplify your landscape, but the contract scope, the responsibility matrix and the transition path all need careful planning.

We help you build the business case, clarify what SAP and the hyperscaler will run versus what remains your responsibility, and then execute the technical and functional transition.

Ask a question
What is included in RISE with SAP?

RISE combines the S/4HANA Cloud licence, hosting on a hyperscaler, technical managed services, SAP Business Technology Platform credits and process intelligence tools. Exact content depends on the edition and the contract you sign.

Is RISE with SAP right for every company?

Not always. It suits organisations that want to move to a subscription model and reduce infrastructure management. We give an independent view, including when a different route may be better.

Can you help after we have signed the RISE contract?

Yes. Many clients engage us after signing to plan and execute the technical transition and to make sure responsibilities not covered by SAP are handled.
