Ask a question
How long does an SAP S/4HANA implementation take?

A focused public-cloud project for a mid-sized company can go live in 4 to 6 months. Larger private-cloud programmes with several entities typically take 9 to 18 months. We give a firm plan after a short discovery phase.

Should we choose the public or private edition of S/4HANA Cloud?

Public edition suits organisations happy to adopt standard processes and receive quarterly updates. Private edition suits complex or highly regulated businesses that need extensions or industry-specific functionality. We assess both against your requirements before you commit.

Do you handle data migration from our current system?

Yes. We extract, cleanse, map and load master and transactional data, run several mock loads and reconcile results with your business owners before go-live.
