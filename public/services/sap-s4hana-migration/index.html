SAP ECC to S/4HANA Migration and Upgrades

Protect years of investment in SAP ECC and move to S/4HANA on a route that suits your timeline, budget and appetite for change.

Talk to an SAP expertCall us

Overview

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC 6.0 ends in 2027, so the question is no longer whether to move to S/4HANA but how. Each approach has different costs, risks and benefits.

Our migration team assesses your system, compares the options with real numbers and delivers the conversion with rehearsed cut-overs and minimal business downtime.

What we offer

Brownfield system conversion

Convert your existing ECC system to S/4HANA, keeping history, configuration and custom code.

Greenfield re-implementation

Start fresh on S/4HANA with redesigned processes and selected data migrated.

Selective data transition

Combine the best of both: carve out entities, merge clients or move only the data you need.

Custom code remediation

Analyse and adapt custom ABAP with SAP tools so it runs on S/4HANA.

Database migration to SAP HANA

Move from Oracle, SQL Server or DB2 to SAP HANA as a first step.

S/4HANA release upgrades

Keep current with the latest S/4HANA release and feature packs.

Frequently asked questions

When does SAP ECC support end?

SAP mainstream maintenance for ECC 6.0 (EHP 6 to 8) ends at the end of 2027, with optional extended maintenance available at extra cost until 2030.

Brownfield or greenfield: which is better?

Brownfield is usually faster and keeps your history; greenfield gives a clean redesign. The right choice depends on how well your current processes serve you, data quality and custom code volume. Our readiness assessment gives a recommendation backed by evidence.

How much downtime does a conversion need?

It depends on database size and the method used. With downtime-optimised conversion, most mid-sized systems can be converted over a weekend.
