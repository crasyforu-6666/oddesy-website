SAP ECC End of Maintenance in 2027: What It Means and What to Do Next

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC ends on 31 December 2027. Here is what changes, what your options are and how to plan a move that does not rush your business.

By the Oddesy Consulting and Services team · 8 Nov 2026

If your business still runs SAP ECC 6.0, the clock is ticking. SAP's mainstream maintenance for SAP Business Suite 7 (including ECC 6.0 enhancement packages 6 to 8) ends on 31 December 2027. After that date, the system will not suddenly stop working, but the support you rely on changes and the cost of standing still starts to rise.

This guide explains what the deadline really means, the options SAP offers, and a realistic plan for moving to SAP S/4HANA without putting your operations at risk.

What "end of mainstream maintenance" actually means

Mainstream maintenance is the standard support included in your SAP maintenance contract. It covers legal and regulatory updates, security patches, support packages and access to SAP support for issues. When it ends, SAP stops delivering new legal changes and fixes for those releases as part of standard support.

For businesses in regulated industries, or in countries where tax and e-invoicing rules change frequently, losing legal updates is often the biggest practical risk.

Your options after 2027

1. Extended maintenance (to end of 2030)

SAP offers optional extended maintenance for ECC 6.0 EHP 6 to 8 until the end of 2030, at an additional fee on top of your existing maintenance. It buys time, but it costs more each year and does not move you closer to S/4HANA.

2. Customer-specific maintenance

Systems not covered by mainstream or extended maintenance fall into customer-specific maintenance. Support continues for known issues, but new legal changes and fixes for new problems are generally no longer delivered. For most businesses this is a risky place to be.

3. RISE with SAP and the private cloud transition option

SAP has also announced transition options for customers who commit to moving to S/4HANA through RISE with SAP, including longer support for ECC running in a private cloud while the move is completed. Terms and eligibility are set by SAP, so confirm the details for your contract before relying on them.

4. Move to SAP S/4HANA

The long-term answer for almost every ECC customer is S/4HANA, either on-premise, in a private cloud through RISE, or in S/4HANA Cloud public edition. The question is not whether to move but how and when.

Why waiting is expensive

  • Consultant demand will peak. Thousands of organisations face the same deadline. Experienced S/4HANA consultants will be harder to find and more expensive the closer you get to 2027.
  • Projects take longer than expected. A typical conversion takes 9 to 18 months, plus time for assessment, budgeting and approvals.
  • Extended maintenance adds cost without adding value. Money spent keeping ECC alive is not spent on improving processes.
  • Technical debt keeps growing. Every new custom development on ECC is another object to analyse and remediate later.

A practical 5-step plan

  1. Run a readiness assessment. Use SAP's Readiness Check and custom code analysis to understand simplification items, add-ons and code that must change.
  2. Choose your migration approach. Brownfield conversion, greenfield re-implementation or selective data transition. See our guide to brownfield vs greenfield.
  3. Decide your deployment model. On-premise, RISE with SAP private cloud or public cloud. Each has different costs and flexibility.
  4. Clean up before you move. Archive old data, retire unused custom code and fix master data quality. This shortens the project and reduces risk.
  5. Plan cut-over early. Agree downtime windows with the business, rehearse with mock conversions and plan hypercare.

Key takeaways

  • ECC 6.0 mainstream maintenance ends on 31 December 2027; optional extended maintenance runs to the end of 2030 at extra cost.
  • Your ECC system keeps running, but legal updates and new fixes become the main risk.
  • Start with an assessment now: it is low cost and gives you the facts to plan budget and timing.

Frequently asked questions

When does SAP ECC mainstream maintenance end?

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC 6.0 enhancement packages 6 to 8 ends on 31 December 2027.

Will SAP ECC stop working after 2027?

No. The system keeps running, but standard support such as legal updates and new fixes changes unless you buy extended maintenance or move to S/4HANA.

How long does an ECC to S/4HANA migration take?

A typical conversion takes 9 to 18 months depending on system size, custom code and the approach chosen, plus time for assessment and planning.
